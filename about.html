A production studio built to sit inside your existing project team.

SB CAD Studio grew from a small drafting desk into a 15–20 member technical production team, without changing how closely we work with each client's in-house architects.

01 — STORY

From a two-person drafting desk to a dedicated production floor.

2023

Studio founded

SB CAD Studio opens as a specialist AutoCAD and millwork detailing desk, taking on shop drawing work for UAE fit-out contractors.

2024

3D CGI division added

A dedicated 3ds Max and V-Ray/Corona rendering team joins the studio, extending the offering from drafting into photorealistic visualization.

2025

US & European standards adopted

The team formalizes AIA and ISO/BS 1192 layering pipelines to serve architects across North America and Europe on the same production floor.

2026

15–20 member production team

The studio now runs parallel drafting and CGI pipelines across three time zones, delivering 500+ drawing sets to date.
